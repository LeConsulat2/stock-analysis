# Stock Research

Produce a decision-useful research note that a non-specialist can check. A supported observation is different from a good investment, and neither establishes suitability for a person's portfolio.

## Establish the Question

Identify the company, listed instrument, question, horizon, and evidence cutoff. Resolve ambiguous company names before attaching financials. If horizon is absent, state a provisional long-term business-research framing. Portfolio weights require portfolio context, not just a ticker.

For supplied commentary, extract its actual thesis and date. Treat newsletters and transcripts as attributed arguments, not verified market data. Do not claim to have watched a video when only its transcript or description is available. Preserve uncertain translation, omitted charts, and missing contract details as gaps.

## Investigate

Use the [company investigator role](../../agents/company-investigator.md) to frame the business questions. Read [research-method.md](references/research-method.md) for evidence fields, valuation conventions, and lessons from this repository's examples.

Browse for current financial claims. Prefer filings, earnings releases, and original industry statistics. Company commentary is primary evidence of what management said, not independent proof of a forecast. Inspect the actual passage behind consequential citations. When originals cannot be obtained, attribute secondary claims and limit the conclusion.

Keep research bounded by the question and user budget. Seek evidence for pivotal assumptions and the strongest plausible contradiction; avoid arbitrary source counts or searching until a preferred answer emerges. If browsing is unavailable, analyze supplied material as of its date and disclose that current verification was unavailable.

Explain the relevant path from demand through supply, pricing, revenue, margins, reinvestment, and cash available to shareholders. Identify which listed entity captures those economics; parents, subsidiaries, customers, and suppliers are not interchangeable.

Use arithmetic or code for calculations, retaining inputs, units, periods, and definitions. Separate reported results, management guidance, external consensus, and assumptions. Consensus targets are not an independently calculated valuation.

## Challenge and Deliver

Apply the [thesis challenger role](../../agents/thesis-challenger.md) after forming the initial case. Run the lens yourself unless delegation is explicitly requested. An AI role is a review procedure, not an independent source or vote.

Use [research-note.md](assets/research-note.md) as an adaptable output template. Answer the question first, then explain evidence, assumptions, disagreement, valuation sensitivity when feasible, and what observation would change the view. Define unfamiliar finance terms where used. Prefer an unresolved question to a forced BUY/HOLD/SELL or invented confidence percentage.

For thesis updates, compare with the dated prior record, separate changed facts from changed interpretation, and preserve the original when saving an authorized update. Without a prior record, establish a baseline rather than inventing history.

This skill supports research in the assistant session. It does not establish that the application implements search, storage, portfolio management, or these roles. It does not authorize trading or publication.
